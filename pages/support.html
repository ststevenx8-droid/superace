Demo Support & FAQ

Is this real money gambling?

No. ST WIN ACE is strictly a virtual coin slot machine simulation created for testing, UX demonstration, and entertainment.

How do I get more coins?

Visit the Wallet page and submit a Virtual Demo Credit request, or reset your local demo credits in the settings menu.

Can I withdraw my coins?

No. Virtual coins hold zero real-world monetary value and cannot be redeemed, transferred, or cashed out.
